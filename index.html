About the Assessment Framework

The Application Classification Assessment uses a proven two-dimensional framework to evaluate applications across Strategic Importance (Y-axis) and Operational Complexity (X-axis).

Based on your responses to 12 key criteria, each application is positioned within one of four strategic quadrants, providing clear guidance on the recommended approach for management and investment.

Low Hanging Fruit

High strategic value, low complexity → Accelerate adoption and investment

Transformation Anchor

High value, high complexity → Plan for careful modernization

Tactical Utility

Low value, low complexity → Tolerate if cost-justified

Containment Liability

Low value, high complexity → Develop exit strategies
